About this Route

The Alpine Crossing is a rigorous but deeply rewarding multi-day trek that traverses some of the most iconic ridges in the Swiss Alps. Expect challenging scree slopes, breathtaking glacial views, and well-maintained but sparse alpine huts. Proper gear and navigation skills are essential.
